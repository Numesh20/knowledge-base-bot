# Remote Work & Equipment Policy

## 1. Remote Work Eligibility
All full-time engineers and product managers are eligible for a hybrid or 100% remote work schedule after completing their 30-day onboarding period.

## 2. Home Office Stipend
Every employee receives a one-time equipment grant of **$1,500** for setting up an ergonomic workstation (monitors, standing desk, ergonomic chair). In addition, an ongoing **$75/month** internet and mobile reimbursement is deposited on the last Friday of each month.

## 3. Working Hours & Availability
Core synchronous collaboration hours are **10:00 AM to 3:00 PM PST**. Outside of these core hours, team members have full flexibility to manage their own schedules.

## 4. Security & Compliance
All company data must remain on encrypted, company-managed laptops. Password managers are mandatory and two-factor authentication (2FA) must be enabled on all corporate accounts.
